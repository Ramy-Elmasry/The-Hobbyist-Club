Welcome to Cycling

Cycling is an outdoor sport and recreational hobby that combines cardio endurance with hands-on mechanical interaction. It can be practiced individually or in groups across different terrains.

Common Cycling Disciplines

  • Road Cycling: Riding lightweight bikes on paved surfaces for distance and speed.
  • Mountain Biking (MTB): Riding on off-road trails, dirt tracks, and rocky terrain.
  • Gravel / Commuting: Versatile riding covering mixed surfaces, city traffic, and long tours.

Basic Riding Guidelines

To enjoy cycling safely, every rider should follow core safety habits:

  1. Always wear a fitted helmet before getting on the bike.
  2. Follow local traffic laws and signaling rules.
  3. Keep tires inflated to the recommended PSI printed on the sidewall.

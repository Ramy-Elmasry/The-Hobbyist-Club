Basic Drivetrain Maintenance

Keeping your bike's drivetrain clean ensures smooth gear shifting and prolongs chain life:

  • Degreasing: Clean dirt and old oil off the chain and cassette cogs.
  • Lubrication: Apply lubricant to chain links, then wipe off excess oil with a rag.
  • Gear Indexing: Adjust cable tension on the derailleur if gears skip under load.

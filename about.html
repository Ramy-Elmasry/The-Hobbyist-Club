About The Project

The Hobbyist Club is a collaborative web development project created for the CSC 111 course at Nile University. Built by a team of six students, this platform brings together our individual hobbies into a cohesive portfolio, applying core HTML semantics and CSS design principles.

Our Goals

Goal 1: Share Hobbies

Provide engaging and informative overviews of diverse personal interests—from cycling and motorsport to basketball, football, and volleyball—complete with equipment guides, rules, and personal insights.

Goal 2: Team Collaboration

Collaborate effectively as a 6-person team to construct a unified 14-page website, maintaining consistent two-way navigation, clean code organization, and shared styling standards.

My race weekend

A Grand Prix is a three-day build-up. This is how I follow one.

Friday to Monday

  1. Friday, practice: teams test setups and tyres. I watch the timing screens and guess who is hiding pace.
  2. Saturday, qualifying: one lap, no margin for error. My favourite hour of the weekend, because the grid decides so much.
  3. Sunday, the race: red shirt on, snacks ready before the formation lap. Phone away until the chequered flag.
  4. Monday, the debrief: replays, onboards and the arguments about whether that overtake was fair.

Want to get into it?

Teams pick tyre compounds to match the race. Here is a simple way to choose how deep you go.

Three ways to follow F1
Level What to do
Soft: quick and fast Watch the race highlights. Ten minutes will show you why people love it.
Medium: steady Watch a full race each week and pick a team to support. Mine is Ferrari, and rooting for someone changes everything.
Hard: go the distance Add qualifying, practice, podcasts and strategy breakdowns. It lasts all season.

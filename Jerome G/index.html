Sunday starts at the lights

Formula 1 is my favourite hobby, and Ferrari is my team. Twenty cars, one start line, and a couple of hours where anything can happen.

I did not grow up around motorsport. I caught one race by accident, kept coming back, and picked a side: Ferrari. Rosso Corsa red, Maranello, and the tifosi filling the grandstands at Monza make every race feel like it matters.

What keeps me hooked

  • The engineering: cars that change shape through a season, pit stops done in about two seconds, and a team of hundreds behind every driver.
  • The strategy: when to pit, which tyre to run, and when to push. A race is often won on the pit wall before it is won on track.
  • The stories: rivalries, comebacks and rookies taking on legends. Ferrari is the oldest team on the grid, and its fans live every season with hope and heartbreak.

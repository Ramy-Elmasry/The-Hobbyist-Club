Why F1 My race weekend

Sunday starts at the lights.

Formula 1 is my favourite hobby, and Ferrari is my team. Twenty cars, one start line, and a couple of hours where anything can happen.

I did not grow up around motorsport. I caught one race by accident, kept coming back, and picked a side: Ferrari. Rosso Corsa red, Maranello, and the tifosi filling the grandstands at Monza make every race feel like it matters.

What keeps me hooked

The engineering

Cars that change shape through a season, pit stops done in about two seconds, and a team of hundreds behind every driver.

The strategy

When to pit, which tyre to run, when to push. A race is often won on the pit wall before it is won on track.

The stories

Rivalries, comebacks and rookies taking on legends. Ferrari is the oldest team on the grid, and its fans live every season with hope and heartbreak.

My race weekend.

A Grand Prix is a three-day build-up. This is how I follow one.

Friday to Sunday

  1. Friday: practiceTeams test setups and tyres. I watch the timing screens and guess who is hiding pace.
  2. Saturday: qualifyingOne lap, no margin for error. My favourite hour of the weekend, because the grid decides so much.
  3. Sunday: the raceRed shirt on, snacks ready before the formation lap. Phone away until the chequered flag.
  4. Monday: the debriefReplays, onboards and the arguments about whether that overtake was fair.

Medium: steady

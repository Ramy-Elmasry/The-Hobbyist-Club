Welcome to Our Project

Created by a team of 6 university students for CSC 111. This website explores our favorite hobbies, bringing together individual passions, guides, and practical insights into one shared portfolio.

Featured Hobbies

Cycling

An exploration of cycling disciplines, essential safety gear checklists, and basic drivetrain maintenance for riders and enthusiasts.

Formula 1

An inside look into Formula 1 racing and Scuderia Ferrari, covering cutting-edge engineering, pit-stop strategy, and race weekend dynamics.

Basketball

A deep dive into the fast-paced world of basketball, highlighting court teamwork, high-energy play, and NBA legend LeBron James.

Volleyball

An introduction to indoor and beach volleyball, highlighting fundamental rules, court setups, essential gear, and team collaboration.

Football

A guide to the world's most popular sport, detailing team structure, tactical gameplay, and why football builds thrilling excitement and camaraderie.

World Football

An introduction to football rules, pitch dimensions, and iconic global competitions like the FIFA World Cup.

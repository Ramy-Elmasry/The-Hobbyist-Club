What is Volleyball?

Volleyball is a team sport played by two teams. Each team tries to send the ball over the net to score points.

Number of Players

Each team has six players on the court in indoor volleyball.

Equipment

Volleyball requires a ball, a net, and a court.

Why I Like Volleyball

I like volleyball because it is fun and teaches teamwork.
